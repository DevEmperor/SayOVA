## SayOVA in one minute

SayOVA is a voice assistant for Wear OS that you talk to like a person. Tap **Talk**, ask anything,
and the answer comes back spoken while you can read along. Speak over it to interrupt, ask a
follow-up, change your mind. It sets timers and alarms, checks the weather, measures your heart rate,
reads your calendar, prepares messages on your phone, interprets between two languages and remembers
what you ask it to — all with **your own API key** from Google or OpenAI, so there is no subscription
and no SayOVA server between you and the AI.

## What you need

- A **Wear OS 3 or newer** watch with a microphone — Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch, TicWatch and others.
- An **API key** from Google AI Studio (recommended, about 1–2 ¢ a minute) or OpenAI.
- A **phone with a camera**, once, to hand the key to the watch. No companion app is needed.
- SayOVA from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.devemperor.sayova).

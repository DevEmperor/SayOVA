# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

First version.

- Realtime conversations with Google Gemini Live (`gemini-3.8-live`, `gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking`)
  and OpenAI Realtime (`gpt-realtime-2.1-mini`, `gpt-realtime-2.1`), with your own API key.
- Interrupt by voice or by tapping the orb; automatic fallback when the watch hears its own speaker.
- Live transcript of both sides, with the heard part of the answer highlighted.
- Silent mode (text answers, push-to-talk).
- Tools: timers, alarms, notes, battery and time, web search.
- QR pairing: the key is encrypted in the browser and relayed end to end to the watch.
- Wi-Fi or LTE during conversations, resumed Gemini sessions after network changes.
- History on the watch, tile, complication, ongoing-activity icon on the watch face.
